# Window materials

The editable casement is in `casement.blend`. The scene includes the packed oak, terrazzo, original glazed highlights and exterior photograph. The presentation camera shows the complete frame.

| Asset | Source | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oiled oak | [Oak Wood Planks, Poly Haven (CC0)](https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_wood_planks) | One continuous board sampled lengthwise along each mitred profile |
| Stone sill | [Terrazzo Tiles, Poly Haven (CC0)](https://polyhaven.com/a/terrazzo_tiles) | Cropped aggregate, desaturated and lightened |
| Exterior | [Shinjuku Station South Entrance at night, calvision (CC0)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:Redirect/page/61307469) | Resized and colour graded for the room |
